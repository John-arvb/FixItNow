How It Works

Getting your equipment repaired is easy — just follow these five simple steps.

  1. Submit Request

    Fill out the repair form with your equipment details and problem description.

  2. Request Reviewed

    Our technician reviews your request and contacts you with an assessment.

  3. Equipment Repaired

    Your device is repaired by our skilled technicians using quality parts.

  4. Status Updated

    You receive an update when your repair is done and ready for pickup.

  5. Completed!

    Pick up your fully repaired equipment — good as new!
